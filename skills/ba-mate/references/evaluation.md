# Optional research practice and feedback

The application-based A/manual, B/generic AI, C/framework comparison remains a separate protocol. Skills use is a portability/formative stream unless a prospective amendment defines its treatment, matched baseline, cases, timing and analysis. Do not pool heterogeneous host/model runs into the application comparison. Public issue counts are not independent participant outcomes; synthetic tests are not practitioner evidence.

Use new public synthetic demonstrations for onboarding. Keep unseen measured cases, facilitator answer sheets, reviewer keys and participant records out of public releases. Actual stakeholder clarification during a study comes from its fixed human facilitator materials, not an improvising LLM.

Use [../assets/run-record.md](../assets/run-record.md) only when participation is voluntary and the collection process permits it. Local BA records, research submission and cloud inference need separate permissions. No telemetry is implemented. Do not send raw project content, paths, names, hashes, backups or responses to GitHub/research collection automatically. Public diagnostic examples must be synthetic or sanitised. Artifact assessment needing private content follows a separately approved transfer, not a public issue attachment.

## Preserved measures

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Clarity | 15 |
| Completeness | 15 |
| Consistency | 10 |
| Testability | 15 |
| Relevance | 10 |
| Transparency | 10 |
| Stakeholder coverage | 10 |
| Traceability | 10 |
| Ethical risk handling | 5 |

Retain original I2-draft.1 assessment semantics: 1 serious deficiency, 3 adequate with limited issues, 5 strong with minor/no observed issues; 2/4 between anchors with an evidence note. QCORE is the mean of the five core dimensions; QRESP is the four responsible dimensions' mean. QWEIGHT sums `(score/5)*weight`: 20–100 when all nine apply, not accuracy and not a 0–100 scale. Any case-level N/A mask must be fixed independently of condition; normalise by applicable weights and report denominators. Do not label these psychometrically validated instruments or use a score as approval.

Record unsupported/contradicted commitments, obligation coverage, material unresolved ambiguity, human-verified trace support/coverage, risk TP/FP/FN, completion/time, corrections and usage when observed. Unknown is missing, not zero. Zero-denominator support/accuracy measures are N/A. Models, tools, context, assistance, previous exposure, failure and version changes affect interpretation. Participant is the analysis unit; requirements/events are not extra people. Negative/null outcomes remain valid.

## Preserved dimension anchors

Adapted from the project's July Stage 2 requirements-quality rubric; P2/I2 controls N/A treatment and index interpretation. Scores 2/4 lie between the adjacent anchors. These descriptions do not establish empirical validation.

| Dimension | 1 — poor | 3 — adequate | 5 — strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clarity | Vague, contradictory or difficult meaning; unclear actor/action/outcome. | Main meaning understandable; one or two terms/relationships need clarification. | Precise, unambiguous meaning understandable to stakeholders/developers. |
| Completeness | Important actors/goals/constraints/acceptance conditions missing. | Main information present; minor omissions/assumptions remain. | Relevant actors/goals/constraints/assumptions/acceptance conditions sufficiently covered. |
| Consistency | Contradictions, incompatible terms or conflicting conditions. | Mostly consistent; minor wording/terminology issues. | Terminology/rules/actors/conditions internally consistent. |
| Testability | No objective verification possible. | Some verification possible; incomplete or subjective criteria. | Specific, observable, objectively verifiable criteria. |
| Relevance | Unrelated content or failure to address stakeholder goal. | Generally relevant; some weakly connected/unnecessary content. | Directly addresses need without unnecessary scope. |
| Transparency | Hidden assumptions/uncertainty presented as facts; absent source connection. | Some visible assumptions; incomplete uncertainty/reasoning limitations. | Clear assumptions/uncertainty/source input/unresolved decisions. |
| Stakeholder coverage | Important affected groups or perspectives ignored. | Primary stakeholder represented; secondary/vulnerable groups may be missing. | Relevant groups and inclusion concerns explicitly considered. |
| Traceability | Cannot connect final artifact to input/decisions. | Partial connection; unexplained important changes/decisions. | Traceable to source, clarification, assumptions and approval decisions. |
| Ethical risk handling | Biased/exclusionary/unsafe/privacy-invasive content without warning. | Risks noticed but incompletely resolved/explained. | Risks identified/explained/reduced where possible and escalated for human review. |

Assess the actual output, not presumed intent or polished wording. Keep original independent ratings, evidence notes and later adjudication separately. Missing required content is a deficiency, not N/A. Calibration and unseen case keys belong to the approved study, not public conformance material.

## Optional feedback items

Use 1 strongly disagree to 5 strongly agree, with not applicable and prefer not to answer:

- F1: I could understand what to do next.
- F2: I could identify which statements still needed confirmation.
- F3: I could review and correct the artifact effectively.
- F4: The effort required was reasonable for this task.
- F5: I would find this approach useful for similar requirements work.

Separately ask perceived confidence in correctness (1 not at all confident to 5 very confident), and an optional comment about what helped/obstructed work, omitting client/company/personal details. Do not compute a validated usability score or equate perceived confidence with accuracy. Feedback must not be a condition of ordinary local use.
