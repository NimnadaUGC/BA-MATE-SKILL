# Artifact templates

Choose the requested deliverable and scale it to the task. These headings are blank templates, not confirmed business facts. Give every output its scope, record IDs/revisions, actual status, origin and limitations. Keep requirements/criteria linked in the local records.

## Requirement / business rule

ID/revision; statement (actor + behaviour/constraint); rationale/value; priority and basis; supporting source/clarification refs and locators; proposed/verified support; assumptions; open decisions; owner; observable criterion IDs/revisions; status/review.

## User story

ID/revision; role; goal; value; linked requirements; criteria; evidence; priority/basis; stakeholder/exception notes; assumptions/questions; status/review. Do not manufacture a persona or business value to fill a template.

## Software Requirements Specification

Title, revision and status; purpose/scope; stakeholders and authority; evidence/context limitations; terminology; functional requirements; nonfunctional requirements and confirmed fit criteria; rules/stories where relevant; acceptance criteria; process description/model; assumptions/risks/open decisions; traceability; review findings; approval manifest/exclusions if recorded; change history.

## Business Requirements Document

Business purpose/outcomes; scope/boundaries; stakeholders; current problem/process and reviewed evidence; business needs/rules; constraints; success criteria with confirmation status; options only when requested; risks/assumptions/open decisions; source/decision trace; review and approval scope.

## Workshop summary

Workshop purpose/date and known roles; source notes/limitations; confirmed decisions with speaker/authority basis; proposed needs; disagreements/unanswered questions and owners; actions; trace references; draft/review status. Attendance or agreement must not be inferred from an AI summary.

## Traceability report

Selected manifest; upstream source/passage and clarification ID/revision; downstream requirement/criterion/model/document ID/revision; relationship; proposed/verified/stale status; human verifier/basis; uncovered commitments; open decisions; limitations. Existing links alone do not prove support.

## Requirements addendum

Prior baseline ID and exact revised scope; change trigger; changed/added/excluded artifacts; before/after revisions; dependent criteria/process/document sections; impacts and human disposition; unresolved matters; new validation and approval record, or draft label.

## Change-impact report

Trigger and supporting evidence; previous baseline/working manifest; potential impacted IDs; affected/unaffected decisions with rationale/owner; stakeholder impacts; proposed revisions; risks and missing information; required revalidation/approval; history preserved.

## Developer handover (optional)

Selected baseline/draft status and revision manifest; requirements/criteria; business process; rules/constraints; traceability; assumptions/open questions; change notes. Technical architecture is unspecified unless expressly present in selected reviewed records. Clearly label technical suggestions as separate proposals.
